Neural Pipeline • Stage 1

Image Classification (MobileNetV2)

Surface-level crack screening fine-tuned on the Utah State University SDNET2018 concrete benchmark.

Architecture Transfer Learning

MobileNetV2 base (ImageNet weights) with custom GlobalAveragePooling2D, Dropout(0.3), and single-unit Sigmoid classifier.

Input Resolution: 224 × 224 × 3

Dataset Grounding SDNET2018

56,092 high-resolution concrete images covering Bridge Decks, Pavements, and Retaining Walls across Utah civil infrastructure.

Balanced class weighting: 0.589 (Intact) / 3.308 (Cracked)

Audit Performance 85.31% Accuracy

ROC-AUC: 0.8881 | Cracked Sensitivity / Recall: 74.06% | Validated on independent test split with strict zero leakage.

Loss: Binary Cross-Entropy | Adam (lr=1e-4)

Neural Pipeline • Stage 2

Crack Segmentation (Symmetrical U-Net)

Pixel-level fissure localization trained on 3,368 cropped CRACK500 pavement image-mask pairs.

Architecture Encoder-Decoder

3-stage symmetrical U-Net with skip connections (64 → 128 → 256 filters) to capture fine concrete micro-fissures.

Input/Output: 256 × 256 × 3 → 256 × 256 × 1

Loss Optimization Combined Loss

Trained with Binary Cross-Entropy + Dice Loss (0.5 × BCE + 0.5 × Dice) to prevent extreme background bias.

Output thresholded at 0.5 → Discrete binary {0, 255}

Surface Density Triage Rule Engine

Automated surface area density calculation: (Crack Pixels / Total Pixels) × 100 with 4 severity levels: None, Low, Moderate, High.

Critical Threshold: > 5.0% surface coverage
